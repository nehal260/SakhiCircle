🌟 Did You Know?

Menstrual cycles can vary greatly among individuals, and what's normal for one person may not be for another. Factors like stress, diet, and exercise can influence cycle length and symptoms. Remember, tracking your cycle helps in early detection of potential health issues. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.

Fun fact: The average menstrual cycle is 28 days, but cycles ranging from 21 to 35 days are considered normal. Stay informed and empowered with SakhiCircle!
